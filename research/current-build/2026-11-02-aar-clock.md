## Verified computation

1. `0xbb04d0`: loads ECX from global pointer slot `0x201d318` and tail-jumps to `0xaaedf0`.
2. `0xaaedf1`: loads the 32-bit integer at ECX+`0xd0` with `movd`.
3. `0xaaedf9`: converts signed integer to float with `cvtdq2ps`.
4. `0xaaedfc`: multiplies by the float at VA `0x195d768`.
5. PE-section-mapped bytes at that VA are `cd cc 4c 3e`, IEEE binary32
   `0.20000000298023224`. This is the binary32 representation of 0.2, not a measured frame duration.
6. `0xaaee04` through `0xaaee0d`: returns the computed float through x87 ST(0).
7. `0xc9f95c` calls `0xbb04d0`; `0xc9f964` saves ST(0), and `0xc9f9ca`
   stores the value into damage module+`0x1ec` on the reached path. An earlier
   guarded return can bypass this store. Read the field on return; do not assume every entry updated it.

Thus the observed computation is `float(signed_counter) * binary32(0.2)`.
Its relationship to simulation time, replay frames, deployment, pause and playback
speed remains unverified. No timestamp is assigned to the prior 28 transitions.
The prior trace did not capture +`0x1ec` or the counter.

## Owner-resolution path

`0xc52de0` adds 8 to ECX and tail-jumps to `0xb00690`. The latter reads
that 32-bit handle, uses its high 16 bits to select a registry record at
`0x2037d34 + high16 * 0x50`, bounds-checks the low 16-bit index against the
record's count at `0x2037d38 + high16 * 0x50`, and obtains a candidate pointer
from its indexed table. It additionally calls `0x5c6aa0` and requires return 1;
otherwise it returns null. This static path does not authenticate a live unit.
Do not reproduce the lookup by calling game functions from a probe.

`0xc9bf20` is the previously identified accumulated-damage threshold check;
`0xa75c70` is its candidate owner/lifecycle follow-up. Their invocation and
same-thread nesting can corroborate control flow, but do not alone prove a
named unit died or identify its shooter. Capture independently verified
identity and outcome evidence before reporting a causal kill.

## Rejected upgrades of evidence

The read-only audit's approximately 5.01 frames/s fit and proposed frame-2914
battle boundary are hypotheses, not captured simulation time. A synchronization
service command is not renamed battle-start. Suppression decay quanta and a
results-screen duration do not establish the clock origin or a timing error bound.
Three damage increases are not authenticated kills without identity and lifecycle evidence.

A module pointer beginning `0x401e...` is outside the image interval
`[0x400000, 0x2268000)` reported for the prior trace, not inside it. This does not
establish whether the allocation is a heap pool or another mapping.

## Lab safeguards

A running-main-menu Parallels snapshot was created and restore-tested. The same
process resumed, but three GDI captures, including after refocus, were black.
A user restart restored visible rendering. The snapshot is retained, but has
not passed running-game rendering restoration. This is not proof of a game crash
or a general Parallels rendering defect.

Standing rule: export every new replay, Frida trace, screenshot and research
evidence file to the Mac, then verify source/host SHA-256 equality before any
baseline restore. Never restore while evidence export or hash verification is incomplete.

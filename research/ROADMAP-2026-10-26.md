## Primary blocker

Not opcode coverage. Validation provenance: one sample source, no replay with
independently known gameplay facts, frame-to-seconds unknown. Every semantic
layer is validated by exact byte consumption and RTTI class names only. A
scripted ground-truth replay would retroactively validate 07/08/0a/0c/0d
semantics, calibrate frame duration and fix the coordinate frame; this is
cheaper and higher-yield than engine playback instrumentation (NEXT-STEPS 2).
